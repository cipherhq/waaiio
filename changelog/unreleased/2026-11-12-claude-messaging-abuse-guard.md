## 2026-11-12 — Stop one customer draining a business's paid messages

- **What changed:** Every bot reply is paid by the business, and one customer could use up its whole month. Owner decision (2026-11-12, "Build A+B"):
  - **Daily limit per customer.** Each business pays for at most 50 bot replies per customer per UTC day (admin default `customer_daily_reply_limit`). A business can set its own limit (dashboard → Settings → Features → Messaging Protection), between 10 and the admin ceiling `customer_daily_reply_limit_max` (500). Over the limit the bot stops answering that customer for that business until the next UTC day and sends one free notice. A customer at a payment step is never cut off. It counts one inbound message once, at its first paid reply. Free platform notices, payment confirmations from webhooks and sends to the business owner do not count.
  - **Repeats ignored.** The same text more than 3 times in a row within 10 minutes (admin `customer_repeat_message_limit`) gets no reply. Taps and media never count as repeats.
  - **Junk pauses.** 5 invalid replies in a row at one flow step (not a payment step) send one "take a short break, then reply *menu*" message and pause the customer for the soft abuse cooldown (5 min).
  - **Pauses hold on every server.** The swearing timeout and junk pause are now stored in the database. Production has no Redis, so the old in-memory timeout reset whenever another server took the message. A paused customer is told once, not on every message.
- **Files:** `supabase/migrations/536_customer_message_guards.sql` (new tables `customer_daily_reply_usage`, `customer_message_guard`, four service-role-only functions, three platform settings), `lib/messaging/customer-message-guard.ts` (new), `lib/channels/message-sender.ts` (guard runs before any attempt row or charge), `app/api/webhook/meta-cloud/route.ts` (sets the guard per inbound message), `lib/bot/bot.service.ts` (inbound guard replaces the in-memory timeout check, stored swearing pause, daily-limit notice), `lib/bot/flows/executor.ts` (junk pause), `lib/platformSettings.ts`, `admin/src/pages/PlatformSettings.tsx`, `app/dashboard/settings/tabs/FeaturesTab.tsx`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (registers the PostgreSQL test). Tests: `lib/__tests__/customer-message-guard.test.ts`, `lib/__tests__/customer-message-guards-536.pg.test.ts`.
- **Affects:** bot replies on every channel, the admin Bot & Automation settings and the merchant Features settings. Billing, prices, spend caps, the financial gate, STOP/START and payment confirmations are unchanged.
- **Could break:** each inbound message adds one database call, and each paid reply's first send adds two (business settings and daily count). A third, the payment-step check, runs only once a customer is over the limit. All guards fail open on a database error (the spend cap still applies). Sends made from a separately resolved channel inside a flow (for example a quote reply on another channel) are not counted. The limit day is UTC, not the business's time zone. Needs migration 536 applied before deploy; without it every guard fails open and nothing is limited.
