## 2026-11-05 — Bot never offers or accepts a date or time that is already taken (#919)

**What changed** (`lib/bot/flows/scheduling.flow.ts`, shared by scheduling, appointment and table reservation)
- **One availability rule** (new `lib/bot/flows/shared/slot-availability.ts`). A start time is open when it passes the time list's existing rule (a booking blocks the start times inside its length, up to the capacity) **and** the booking function's rule: `book_slot_atomic` (M477) — exact-time capacity, and with a service buffer no overlapping booking at another time; `book_table_atomic` (M510) — no overlap on the table, including a booking from the night before. It only removes times; nothing the list hid before is shown.
- **Time list** (`select_time.prompt`) builds its times through the new `candidateTimes()` / `openTimesForDate()` (the slot code moved there unchanged) and that rule. Times the booking function would refuse (e.g. 09:00 for a 60-min service with a 15-min buffer before a 10:00 booking; 18:30 before a 19:00 table reservation) are no longer shown.
- **Typed or tapped time** (`select_time.validate`): must be inside the opening hours and free by the same rule (a time between list times, e.g. "10am" on a 45-min list, is judged the same way and still accepted when free). Before, only bookings at that exact minute were checked, so "10:30" inside a 60-min 10:00 booking, or "3am", was accepted. Booked: `booking.time_taken` / the table message, as before. Outside the hours (new message): "That time isn't available. Please pick a time from the list."
- **Pre-filled time** (`select_time.skipIf`): a booked or out-of-hours time now shows the time list (it was skipped).
- **Tables whose business hours close at or after midnight** (`candidateTimes`, close filter): closing at 00:00 or 02:00 now runs into the next day, as `book_table_atomic` (M510) reads it. Before, "00:00" counted as 0 and every evening time was dropped from the time list. A table's own closing time (`available_to`) is still compared as it is, like `book_table_atomic`.
- **Date list, tapped / typed date** (`collectOpenDates`, #916): a date is shown only when the time list would have a time on it. Before, the day count used `_service_duration`, a key that is never set, so every service was counted in 30-min slots: a 60-min service with every hour booked still showed the day. The exact check is used for tables and services without staff. For services with staff the old per-day count is kept: capacity is per staff member and the staff step runs again after every date (a staff member kept from before "Change date" is not final).
- **Pre-filled date** (`select_date.skipIf`): a fully booked pre-filled date now shows the date list (bookings were not checked).
- **Taken at the last moment** (`create_booking`): when `book_slot_atomic`, `book_table_atomic` or the package claim refuses the time, the customer gets "Sorry, this time slot just got booked. Please choose another time." (existing `booking.time_taken`, all locales) and the open times on that date again (or "fully booked" with "Pick another date"). A new time goes back to the summary to confirm, then straight to booking; name, referral and email are not asked again. Before: "Send *Hi* to pick a different time" and the next reply ended the flow. After 3 refusals in one session the old message is kept (no loop). Reaching the booking step again before a new time is picked and confirmed (e.g. "back" on the re-shown summary, or after "Change date") never books: the time list (or date list) is shown again. Classes keep their own full/waitlist messages.
- New session keys `_slot_taken_return`, `_slot_taken_count`; cleared on a capability switch (`lib/bot/flows/shared/capability-transition.ts`).

**Unchanged**: `book_slot_atomic` / `book_table_atomic` (the authority), classes, drop-off services, property reservations (`reservation.flow.ts`), payments and price authority, #916 paging and typed dates, quick date buttons, waitlist. No migration.

**Tests**: `lib/__tests__/bot-unavailable-dates-919.test.ts` (the rule; real `select_date` / `select_time` / `confirmation` / `create_booking` steps with a frozen clock; the real `FlowExecutor` for a refused claim). Source-text checks in `p1-staff-booking-authority.test.ts` (tests 21 and 40) and `table-reservation-730.test.ts` (2 tests) now point at the moved code.

**What could break**
- Fewer times can be shown: with a buffer, times whose length runs into a later booking; for tables, times that run into a later reservation or follow one from the night before.
- More table times can be shown: evenings for businesses that close at or after midnight (they were all dropped).
- A typed time inside a booking or outside the hours is now refused.
- A day can disappear from the date list sooner for tables and services without staff (it is judged by the real time list). Days for services with staff are judged as before.
- When `book_slot_atomic` refuses for a reason the list cannot see (e.g. "Any available" on a service that requires staff), the customer is told the time was just booked up to twice before the old message.
- The time step and a pre-filled date now read the staff schedule / bookings once more; the date list judges each day without extra queries.
