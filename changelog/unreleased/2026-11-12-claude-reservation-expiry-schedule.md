## 2026-11-12 — The messaging reservation-expiry job now runs hourly (#1000)

- **What changed:** `/api/cron/reservation-expiry` was added in #302 but never scheduled, and nothing else did its job (`recover-reservations` only handles growth-credit campaign reservations). A send that stopped between authorization and sending left its reservation holding the merchant's prepaid messaging balance and spend-cap room indefinitely. It now runs hourly at :50 (Owner decision 2026-11-12). The route and `safe_release_expired_reservation` (M371) are unchanged: they release only attempts still `pending_authorization`, with no WhatsApp message id, not flagged and past their deadline, and flag everything else for reconciliation.
- **Files:** `vercel.json`; test `lib/__tests__/reservation-expiry-cron-schedule.test.ts`.
- **Affects:** merchant messaging balances and monthly spend-cap usage, which are given back when a reservation expires unsent. Production had 0 stuck reservations on 2026-11-12. Staging has 4 (US$0.04, "Waaiio test", 2026-10-07): after deploy, 3 are released and 1 ambiguous attempt stays flagged for an admin.
- **Could break:** nothing new runs in the send path. A bug in the existing route or function would now actually execute hourly; the function rechecks every safety condition under a row lock, and a trigger blocks a later send on a released reservation.
